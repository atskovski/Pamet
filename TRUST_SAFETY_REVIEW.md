# Insight trust-and-safety release gate

Every new observation, pattern, trend, Visit Brief, or generated health message must pass this review before release:

- It describes logged association or timing, not diagnosis or causation.
- It does not prescribe treatment, medication changes, urgency, or emergency action.
- It shows the time window, sample size/data strength, and material limitations.
- It uses neutral language and makes uncertainty visible.
- It behaves safely with sparse, missing, contradictory, and extreme data.
- It does not expose another profile's data or expand a sharing permission.
- A product reviewer and a clinically qualified reviewer approve the exact user-facing copy when the feature could reasonably influence care decisions.

Automated tests are necessary but cannot replace clinical, legal, privacy, accessibility, or security review.
